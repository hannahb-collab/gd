HKDSE Group Discussion Useful Expressions Bank

1. Opening the Discussion

  • "Good morning / afternoon everyone. Shall we start our discussion today?"
  • "To kick off our discussion today, let's first look at..."
  • "Why don't we begin by discussing the first point, which is..."

2. Expressing Agreement & Building On Points

  • "I completely agree with Candidate A's point that... Furthermore,..."
  • "That's a very valid point made by Candidate B. Building on that, I think..."

3. Expressing Polite Disagreement

  • "I see your point, but have you considered that..."
  • "While I understand Candidate A's perspective, I am slightly concerned that..."
